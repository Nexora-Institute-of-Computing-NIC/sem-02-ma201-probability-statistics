# MA201 — Probability & Statistics — PLAN

**Semester:** 2 · **Credits:** 4 · **Level:** Beginner-Intermediate
**Theory:** 45 hrs · **Tutorial:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Python (NumPy, SciPy, matplotlib, pandas)

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Probability Foundations (9 lectures)
1. Sample spaces and events
2. Axioms of probability
3. Conditional probability
4. Independence
5. Bayes' theorem
6. Law of total probability
7. Combinatorics in probability — permutations, combinations
8. The Monty Hall problem and its variants
9. Common probability pitfalls — gambler's fallacy, base-rate neglect

### Unit 2 — Random Variables (9 lectures)
1. Discrete random variables
2. Probability mass functions (PMF)
3. Cumulative distribution functions (CDF)
4. Continuous random variables — density functions
5. Expectation and variance
6. Common discrete distributions — Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson
7. Common continuous distributions — Uniform, Exponential
8. The Normal distribution and the Central Limit Theorem
9. Joint distributions and covariance

### Unit 3 — Statistical Inference (9 lectures)
1. Populations, samples, and sampling
2. Sampling distributions
3. Point estimation — mean, variance, proportion
4. Bias, efficiency, consistency
5. Maximum likelihood estimation
6. Confidence intervals — for mean and proportion
7. The t-distribution and small samples
8. Hypothesis testing — null, alternative, p-value
9. Type I vs Type II errors and statistical power

### Unit 4 — Regression and Correlation (9 lectures)
1. Scatterplots and correlation
2. Pearson and Spearman correlation
3. Simple linear regression — least squares
4. Interpreting regression coefficients
5. R-squared and goodness of fit
6. Multiple linear regression
7. Residual analysis
8. Multivariate relationships — confounding, interaction
9. Logistic regression (introduction)

### Unit 5 — Applications in Computer Science (9 lectures)
1. Probability in randomised algorithms
2. Hashing and the birthday paradox
3. Bloom filters — probabilistic data structures
4. Markov chains and PageRank
5. Monte Carlo methods
6. A/B testing in product design
7. Bayesian thinking for ML
8. Naive Bayes classifiers
9. Evaluating machine learning models — precision, recall, ROC

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Simulate dice rolls and verify convergence to expected values
2. Simulate the Monty Hall problem
3. Combinatorics calculator — permutations and combinations
4. Bayes' theorem via simulation
5. Sample from Binomial, Poisson, and Normal distributions
6. Plot and analyse PMF, PDF, CDF
7. Calculate expectation and variance — analytic vs simulated
8. Central Limit Theorem demonstration
9. Confidence intervals in Python with SciPy
10. Hypothesis testing — t-test on real data
11. A/B test analysis on a simulated dataset
12. Simple linear regression with NumPy
13. Residual diagnostics and prediction intervals
14. Bloom filter implementation and false-positive rate
15. Naive Bayes classifier on the Iris dataset
